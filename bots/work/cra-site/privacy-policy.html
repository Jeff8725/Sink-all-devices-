SMS Privacy Policy

Program name: Citizens Ratification Inc.
Effective date: September 7, 2026
Last updated: September 7, 2026

This SMS Privacy Policy explains how Citizens Ratification Inc. (“we,” “us,” or “CRI”) collects, uses, and protects mobile information in connection with our text messaging program. By opting in to receive texts from Citizens Ratification Inc., you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Who we are

Citizens Ratification Inc. is a temporary nonpartisan nonprofit corporation advancing education and public information about the Citizens Ratification Amendment. Corporate contact:

  • CEO: Jeff Gunson
  • Phone: (903) 646-1735
  • Email: JeffGunson@hotmail.com
  • Website: citizensratification.com

2. Information we collect

When you opt in to the Citizens Ratification Inc. SMS program, we may collect and store:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Opt-in / opt-out status and related consent records (for example, the time and method of consent)
  • Message delivery and interaction data needed to operate the program (for example, delivery status and keyword replies such as STOP or HELP)
  • Any information you voluntarily include in a text reply to us

We do not require you to provide full personal profiles, Social Security numbers, or payment card data to receive SMS updates.

3. How we use mobile information

We use mobile phone numbers and related SMS data only to:

  • Send program messages you consented to receive from Citizens Ratification Inc.
  • Confirm opt-in, provide HELP information, and process STOP / opt-out requests
  • Operate, secure, and improve the messaging program and meet carrier and legal compliance requirements

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

4. Sharing of mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We do not sell mobile phone numbers. We may share mobile information only as needed with:

  • Messaging service providers and carriers that transmit texts on our behalf (for example, our SMS platform vendor), solely to deliver and support the program
  • Service providers who help us operate our systems under confidentiality obligations
  • Authorities when required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, or integrity

Mobile opt-in data is not shared with third parties for those third parties’ own marketing.

5. Opt-out, HELP, and choices

You can stop receiving SMS messages from Citizens Ratification Inc. at any time:

  • STOP: Reply STOP to cancel. You will receive a one-time confirmation, and we will stop sending program texts to that number.
  • HELP: Reply HELP for help. You will receive contact information for assistance.

You may also email JeffGunson@hotmail.com or call (903) 646-1735 to request removal. Standard message and data rates may apply to STOP and HELP replies.

6. Data retention and security

We retain mobile numbers and consent records for as long as needed to operate the SMS program, honor opt-outs, and meet legal, carrier, and audit requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children’s privacy

The Citizens Ratification Inc. SMS program is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect mobile information from children under 13. If you believe we have received such information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this SMS Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued participation in the SMS program after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy, except where additional consent is required by law.

9. Related terms and contact

SMS program terms are stated in our Terms and Conditions. General site privacy practices are also summarized on our About page.
