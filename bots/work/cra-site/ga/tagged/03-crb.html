03 – CRB

The permanent, coequal, and strictly limited fourth branch of government that will administer the national ratification Portal after the Amendment is ratified. Its sole function is to administer citizen ratification — nothing more.

03.01 What Is the CRB

Permanent independent fourth branch created by the Amendment.

03.02 Sole Function

Administer citizen ratification only.

03.03 Governance Structure

Director appointed by President. Board of one member per state Governor. No CRB payroll.

03.04 No-Employee Contractor Model

Zero employees. All work by contracts terminable at will without cause or penalty.

03.05 The CRB Portal

Secure national interface. Strong authentication. Minimal data storage.

03.06 Sentinel AI

Public neutral AI. Factual summaries only. No power over outcomes.

03.07 Master Law Library Operational

Sole record of enforceability. Searchable by category, jurisdiction, date.

03.08 Citizen Commons Operational

Open discussion. Full free speech. User filtering.

03.09 Funding Model in Operation

Mandatory Treasury money only. Scales with registered citizens. No outside funds.

03.10 Anti-Tampering Enforcement

Immediate investigation. DOJ referral. 20 years to life for anyone.

03.11 Continuity, Security, and Redundancy

100 % recoverability. Multi-region and offline protections.

03.12 Educational and Modeling Tools

Free lifelong education and modeling tools for all citizens.

03.13 Investigative Boards

Random five-member boards from state appointees.

03.14 Prohibited Actions

Zero tolerance. No delay, influence, or reinterpretation of citizen will.
